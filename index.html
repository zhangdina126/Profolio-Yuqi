01 — Soft Boundaries

Womenswear · Textile & Print

BRERA / GRADUATE COLLECTION / 2026

Care, protection and the intimacy of textiles.

A seven-look collection drawing on the fabrics that covered objects in Chinese homes during the 1980s and 1990s: television covers, curtains, tablecloths and pillowcases. Embroidery, lace, print and fabric manipulation translate domestic memories into womenswear, exploring the tension between care and control.

Developed through material research, draping, sampling and garment construction. Selected looks were presented at Devil Wears Brera 2026; the collection was featured by Vogue Italia and WWD Japan.

02 — Dedar Milano

Textile & Surface Design

THE GARDEN IN A ROOM / 2023–24

From textile surface to interior atmosphere.

An academic concept proposal exploring interior textiles and surface experimentation in the context of Dedar Milano. The project brings together material research and visual development to consider how textiles can shape the character of a room.

Focus: textile research, surface design and material experimentation.

03 — IOC × Milano Cortina 2026

Outerwear · Material Research

THE TRACES OF SILENCE / ACADEMIC COLLABORATION / 2026

Traces of movement, freedom and order.

Inspired by archival imagery of Cortina 1956, this project explores the relationship between freedom and order through a reversible down jacket, a quilted skirt and a hat. Material research brings together Toray fabric, goose down and Leadford Logan wool to explore volume, lightness and protection. The completed look combines outerwear design, material research and garment construction.

Presented at Dazi Sempione in Milan on 4 February 2026 as part of the IOC × Milano Cortina 2026 × Brera project.
